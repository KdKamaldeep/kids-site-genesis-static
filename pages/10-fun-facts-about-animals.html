🌟 10 Fun Facts About Animals

Dolphins sleep with one half of their brain awake and one eye open. An octopus possesses three hearts, two pumping blood through gills and one for the body. Koalas have unique fingerprints that are nearly indistinguishable from human ones. Sloths are incredibly slow, taking up to two weeks to fully digest a single meal. Chameleons change their skin color not only for camouflage but also to express mood and temperature. Some frogs can freeze solid during winter and then completely thaw out and revive. Giraffes have remarkably long, dark blue tongues which protect them from sun exposure while eating. Wombats are the only animals known to naturally produce distinctive cube-shaped feces. Butterflies taste their food by standing on it, using special receptors on their feet. Honeybees are surprisingly intelligent and can even recognize individual human faces.
