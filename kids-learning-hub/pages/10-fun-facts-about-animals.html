🌟 10 Amazing Animal Facts You Never Knew

Octopuses possess three hearts; two pump blood through the gills, and one circulates it to the rest of the body. Sloths are incredibly slow, taking weeks to digest a single meal, making them the slowest mammals. Giraffes, despite their long necks, have the same number of neck vertebrae as humans: just seven. Kangaroos cannot walk backward due to the structure of their powerful legs and tails. Bats are the only mammals capable of true, sustained flight, utilizing specialized wing membranes. A group of owls is collectively known as a parliament, a fitting term for their wise appearance. Dolphins sleep with one half of their brain awake and one eye open to watch for predators. Chameleons change color not only for camouflage but also to express their mood and body temperature. Frogs do not drink water through their mouths; instead, they absorb it directly through their permeable skin. Hummingbirds are unique among birds for their ability to fly backward, a feat unmatched by others.
